About

I'm a staff software engineer at iFood, based in Belo Horizonte, working on ledger solutions. I studied Electrical Engineering at UFMG, with a minor in Computer Science, and I've spent most of my career in fintech.

I worked on Open Finance at Inter, then moved to Mercado Livre to work on their payment gateway, and today I build the ledgers that keep track of where the money is.

While most of my time is spent in front of the PC, I also love writing about software engineering and reading a good book. The Lord of the Rings is my all-time favorite, and I'll gladly rewatch the movies too. I also enjoy A Game of Thrones and anything by Haruki Murakami.

When I'm not reading, I'm usually watching a movie or gaming. Final Fantasy VII and X are close to my heart, and I'm a fan of Metal Gear and Baldur's Gate III. When I have some spare time, I also play Magic: The Gathering or RPGs with my friends.
